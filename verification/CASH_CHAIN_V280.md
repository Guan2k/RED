# v0.280 legal cash-chain evidence

Run `node verification/cash-chain.cjs` separately. Product source and version are unchanged; original regression and long-game runners are unchanged.

A normal classic opening, seed 3280, follows one 19.5-second legal UI cash chain: pay 180 for a scout, advance half a second and cancel for 157; pay 300 through the coordinate construction UI for a power plant at (80,410), advance one second and cancel for 240; pay 300 again at the same site, wait for actual completion, then salvage for 150. Order and construction refunds reduce net spent. Completed-building salvage increases income. Ore deliveries continue naturally. Final cash is 2007 = initial 1700 + income 690 - net spent 383. Both teams' ledgers are checked throughout.

127 assertions / 26 checkpoints pass. Cancellation and salvage confirmations are first rejected with the complete core unchanged, then accepted by a new explicit click. A separate modeled confirmation callback legally changes selection to HQ before acceptance; the complete post-selection core must remain unchanged. Old-order pointerup/click replay cannot refund twice. Repeated direct building handlers with empty selection do not settle again. After another 12 seconds, both retired buildings remain absent and the canceled order has neither produced nor requeued. The replacement building is a distinct object and ID and completes naturally.

Confirmation responses and selection change during a dialog are isolated synthetic VM callback models, not native-dialog acceptance. Core comparisons omit only state.logs because rejection legitimately records feedback. All gameplay actions use normal UI controls/map events; only updateUI is called as a normal projection refresh, and other model queries are read-only. No resource, HP, position, entity or result injection; no save/load/import/export or browser actions. No claim of a common building-input activation token is made.

Existing production-mode-natural and production-gesture-natural already cover order cancellation/repeated settlement and retired-order late releases extensively. This supplement fills the continuous paid-construction cancellation/rebuild/salvage ledger gap, rather than repeating those matrices. The initial driver incorrectly included feedback logs in a no-change assertion; that failed attempt is retained outside the package and is not a product defect.
